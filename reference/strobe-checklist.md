# STROBE — reporting standard for observational studies

The ESI study is a retrospective cohort. STROBE is the applicable checklist and
most reproductive-medicine journals, including Fertility & Reproduction, expect
adherence.

Official checklist: https://www.strobe-statement.org/

## The 22 items

| # | Section | Item |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title and abstract | Indicate the design in the title or abstract; give a balanced summary |
| 2 | Introduction — background | Scientific background and rationale |
| 3 | Introduction — objectives | State specific objectives, including prespecified hypotheses |
| 4 | Methods — design | Present key design elements early |
| 5 | Methods — setting | Setting, locations, recruitment/exposure/follow-up/collection dates |
| 6 | Methods — participants | Eligibility criteria, sources, selection methods |
| 7 | Methods — variables | Define outcomes, exposures, predictors, confounders, effect modifiers |
| 8 | Methods — data sources | Sources and measurement methods for each variable; comparability across groups |
| 9 | Methods — bias | Efforts to address potential sources of bias |
| 10 | Methods — study size | How study size was arrived at |
| 11 | Methods — quantitative variables | How handled; which groupings and why |
| 12 | Methods — statistical methods | Including confounder control, subgroups, missing data, sensitivity analyses |
| 13 | Results — participants | Numbers at each stage; reasons for non-participation; flow diagram |
| 14 | Results — descriptive data | Characteristics; missing data per variable |
| 15 | Results — outcome data | Number of outcome events |
| 16 | Results — main results | Unadjusted and adjusted estimates with precision; confounders adjusted for and why; category boundaries; translate relative to absolute risk where meaningful |
| 17 | Results — other analyses | Subgroups, interactions, sensitivity analyses |
| 18 | Discussion — key results | Summarise with reference to objectives |
| 19 | Discussion — limitations | Sources of bias/imprecision, direction and magnitude |
| 20 | Discussion — interpretation | Cautious overall interpretation |
| 21 | Discussion — generalisability | External validity |
| 22 | Other | Funding and role of funders |

## Items this study is most likely to fail

- **#10 study size** — the handoff gives a practical target of 1,500–2,000 cycles,
  not a power calculation. A statistician must supply the real one.
- **#12 missing data** — must be described, not silently dropped.
- **#13 flow diagram** — Figure 1. Every exclusion, with counts.
- **#14 missing per variable** — the pilot should establish this early.
- **#16 absolute risk** — an adjusted odds ratio alone is weak reporting for a
  clinical audience. Give absolute live-birth probabilities across the ESI range.
- **#17** — subgroups must be flagged as prespecified or post-hoc.
