# Literature scan

For the ESI study this is not optional background work. The handoff is explicit:
the paper must **not** claim dynamic endometrial change has never been studied. It
has, with conflicting results. The evidence table is what lets the novelty claim be
stated honestly and survive review.

## Procedure

1. Search for prior work on endometrial compaction, endometrial thickness change
   after progesterone, and transfer-day endometrial measurement in FET.
2. For each study, extract into `studies/esi-fet/literature/evidence-table.md`:
   author, year, design, N, population (SET/DET, blastocyst/cleavage, euploid),
   how change was measured and defined, endpoint used, adjustment performed,
   finding, and limitations.
3. Sort by whether the finding was positive, null, or mixed. The conflict in this
   literature is the story — make it visible.
4. Identify what is genuinely unaddressed: which studies used live birth, which
   adjusted for embryo quality, which modelled change continuously, which included
   euploid-only cohorts.

## Rules

- **Every entry needs a verifiable source.** Mark anything unconfirmed as
  `[UNVERIFIED]` rather than presenting it as established. See `CLAUDE.md` rule 2.
- Do not summarise a study you have not actually retrieved.
- Record the search strategy and date — a manuscript will need it.
