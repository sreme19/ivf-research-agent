## Parallel tracks — start these now, they do not wait on data

1. **Get the official abstract guidelines.** Not published on either ASPIRE site.
   Email `abstract@aspire2027.com`. Draft sitting in
   `venues/aspire-2027/guidelines/email-draft.md`. Until this lands, nobody knows
   the word limit, the required structure, whether structured abstracts are
   mandatory, or the prior-publication policy.
2. **Ethics / IRB — this is no longer parallel, it is the critical path.**
   Submission to the Indira IVF IEC has not been made. Retrospective chart review
   often qualifies for expedited review or a consent waiver, but the determination
   must be on file before extraction, and turnaround depends on the committee's
   meeting schedule rather than on how fast anyone works. See
   `studies/esi-fet/SITE-AND-GOVERNANCE.md` for what to confirm.
3. **Literature scan.** Build the evidence table of prior compaction and
   remodeling studies. This is what scopes the novelty claim honestly, and it is
   needed for the abstract's framing regardless of what the data shows. Run the
   `literature-scan` skill.

Tracks 2 and 3 are the ones most likely to be underestimated.

## Fallbacks, if the ET2 gate fails

Decide by 15 September, not in late October.

- **Submit a different study** to ASPIRE 2027 from existing complete data, and run
  ESI prospectively for a later venue.
- **Submit ESI as a prospective protocol / methods abstract** — some congresses
  accept study-design submissions; whether ASPIRE does is unknown until the
  guidelines arrive.
- **Skip ASPIRE 2027**, run ESI properly and prospectively, and target a journal
  plus a 2028 congress. The study is stronger this way. A rushed retrospective
  with 40% missing ET2 will not survive review anywhere.

The third option is not a failure. It is the correct call if the data is not there.
