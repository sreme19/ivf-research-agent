## Programme notes

From the congress site: a scientific programme covering key developments in the
field, Special Interest Group sessions on emerging areas, and a guest appearance
marking nearly 50 years of IVF.

The theme's framing — established principle balanced against innovation — is worth
noting for abstract positioning. A study asking whether a *newer* dynamic measure
adds anything beyond an *established* static one sits naturally against it.
