# ASPIRE abstract rules

**Status: INHERITED, NOT OFFICIAL FOR 2027.**

ASPIRE 2027 has not published its guidelines. Everything below is taken from the
**ASPIRE 2026 (Beijing)** call for abstracts, corroborated against **ASPIRE 2025**
and **ASPIRE 2024**, which carried identical title and word limits. The rules have
been stable across at least three consecutive congresses, so they are a sound
working basis — but every rule here must be re-verified against the official 2027
document before submission.

## HARD LIMITS

| Rule | Value | Verified for 2027? |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | **25 words maximum, in UPPER CASE** | No — inherited |
| **Abstract body** | **300 words maximum, INCLUDING acknowledgements** | No — inherited |
| **Abstracts per author** | **2 maximum** | No — inherited |
| Tables | Max 3, each ≤10 rows × 10 columns, preferably submitted as JPG images | No — inherited |
| Images | Max 2, ≤500 KB each, 600 (w) × 800 (h) px, JPG / PNG / GIF | No — inherited |

Word-processing software is recommended for editing and counting words.

## REQUIRED STRUCTURE

The abstract must clearly state, in this order:

1. **Background and Aims**
2. **Methods**
3. **Results**
4. **Conclusions**

This is a mandatory four-part structure, not a suggestion. An unstructured
narrative abstract does not comply.

## PRESENTATION TYPES

Submissions are invited for **oral and/or poster** presentation. The Scientific
Committee assigns the format.

## PRIOR PRESENTATION AND PUBLICATION

- Work previously presented at a **national or regional** conference **remains
  eligible**, provided this is disclosed.
- Prior presentation at an **international** meeting **may preclude acceptance**,
  especially if the work has been published. ASPIRE may still accept it if it is
  unpublished and the prior presentation is disclosed.
- Abstracts **accepted for publication but not yet published** remain eligible.

## PRESENTING AUTHOR

The presenting author is required to complete registration by the stipulated
deadline. Failure to register can result in withdrawal of the accepted abstract
from the programme.

## NOTIFICATION

ASPIRE 2026 notified authors in **January** for a **May** congress, from a
**November** deadline. On the same cadence, ASPIRE 2027 decisions should be
expected around **January 2027**.

## AWARDS (ASPIRE 2026 categories)

| Award | Eligibility | Selection | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Best Oral / Best Poster** | All presenters | 10 orals and 10 posters shortlisted for onsite judging; 5 winners per category | USD 200 book prize, certificate |
| Psychology and Counselling Award | Psychologist, counsellor or social worker; psychosocial fertility care topic | 1 awardee | USD 150, certificate |
| Nursing Award | Nurse, midwife or student in those professions; Nursing category | 1 awardee | USD 150, certificate |

**The Young Investigator Award is not being pursued** (SPEC.md Amendment 2).
Best Oral / Best Poster remains relevant and requires no action — it is open to all
presenters and shortlisting is done by the Scientific Committee.
