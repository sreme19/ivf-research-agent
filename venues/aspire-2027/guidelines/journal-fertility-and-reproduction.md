## Scope

Reproductive medicine and surgery: assisted reproductive technology (IVF, medical
assisted reproduction), andrology, embryology, fertility preservation, early
pregnancy and birth outcomes after fertility treatment, reproductive genetics,
contraception, sexual health, endometriosis and adenomyosis, transgender and
midlife medicine, ethics and legal aspects, and AI applications.

An ESI cohort study sits squarely in scope.

## Article types

- Original research — clinical and basic science
- Review articles
- Case reports, if judged of widespread clinical interest
- Prospective clinical trials, **including negative results**

That last point matters: the journal explicitly states it will consider trials
"whether the results are positive or negative." A rigorous null ESI finding has a
home here.

## Strategic note

Publishing the full paper in ASPIRE's own journal after presenting at the ASPIRE
congress is a coherent pathway, and congress presentation does not jeopardise
journal submission. It is not the only option — a higher-impact reproductive
medicine journal is worth considering depending on how the results land — but F&R
is the low-friction default and its Scopus indexing makes it a real publication,
not just a proceedings dump.
